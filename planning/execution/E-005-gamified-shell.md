The Roblox game (`game/`) becomes the gamified front end of the loop.
Design direction confirmed by Drew (2026-08-15):

- **Hub + personal workspace.** A shared hub for common services (like
  "Grow Your Garden": buy seeds, buy gear, sell produce) and each player's
  own space where they build. Bird's-eye view of the landscape: hub + one
  player's workspace.
- The idea flow is gamified: grab an idea (from the Idea Finder), take it
  to your workspace, start building.
- Fusion UI framework already installed via Wally.
- The game is the delivery vehicle — the real purpose is producing real
  projects, simulated in the harness first.
